Token Radar

Which protocol is worth farming next, and what to do about it. Every protocol below records no token yet in the snapshot; each is ranked by the implied value its launch would carry if its record stayed as it is. That value is this radar’s own claim — not a price, and not a prediction that a token will ever launch.

How the value is worked out

implied = TVL × 0.25 × (1 + clamp(7-day change, −60, +120) ÷ 100 × 0.5) × (0.6 + min(age, 1095) ÷ 1095 × 0.4) × (1 + 0.02 × min(chains − 1, 5))

TVL is the protocol’s locked value in dollars. The 7-day change is its TVL growth over seven days, clamped so one wild week cannot run the board. Age is the days it has been tracked with no token recorded (365 days when DefiLlama gives no listing date). Chains counts the chains it runs on. A protocol whose token already trades is never ranked — the 24 largest of those are set aside at the bottom, shown with their market caps. The same 7-day change also drives the growth signals on every row: the dollars the TVL gained or lost in seven days, a momentum index from 0 to 100, and a tier from Surging (+20% or more) to Falling (−10% or worse).
